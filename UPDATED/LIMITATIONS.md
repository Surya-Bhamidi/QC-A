# Limitations and unresolved acceptance criteria

**October 2 follow-up:** [BIAS_AWARE_RESULTS.md](BIAS_AWARE_RESULTS.md) adds ten training seeds, five measurement repetitions, measured finite calibration costs and complete-test noisy final-block CLS attention across 2,400 conditions. It uses exact factorized density matrices and finite parity sampling, verified against full eight-wire circuits. The joint method fails against strong controls. This closes those narrower evidence gaps; it does not supply end-to-end noisy inference, entangling features, hardware, external-patient validation or established novelty. The numbered list below describes the original experiment and remains applicable to that scope.

**Earlier accuracy attempts:** longer training and a convolutional encoder were compared under matched recipes. Neither improves fidelity accuracy over the original 87.85% mean. The hybrid's validation-to-test gap is observed; its cause is not established by the change in class prevalence alone. The post hoc calibration audit uses previously inspected test data and target-batch statistics for EM/BBSE; its 88.94% ensemble result needs independent confirmation and does not establish kernel superiority. These earlier studies did not achieve greater-than-90% test accuracy.

**Quantum-preserving follow-ups:** trained four-qubit entangling attention and complete-test ideal compute-uncompute inference are now executed in separate accuracy studies. The original small backbone reaches 89.74% with a product ensemble; frozen ImageNet features from upsampled 28-pixel images give 86.70% quantum ensemble accuracy. The completed native-224 branch reaches 91.60% mean / 91.67% ensemble for entangled fidelity; matched dot has a 92.15% ensemble. All thirty quantum test classifiers are independently executed with PennyLane. These close the narrower entangling-training and full-test ideal-circuit gaps for those new architectures. They do not establish end-to-end noisy/finite-shot accuracy, physical execution, kernel superiority or algorithmic novelty. The native-224 gain uses external pretraining and higher-resolution inputs, changing the model/data regime. All outcomes share a previously inspected cohort and remain exploratory. The older numbered limitations below retain their original scope.

1. **Novelty has not been established.** HQViT already uses SWAP attention, destructive attention exists, and adaptive sensitivity/variance quantum-kernel allocation is close prior art. The attention-output specialization is an empirical hypothesis. The current negative result is not proof of a new algorithmic contribution. Public searches across the requested venues were conducted, but citation-graph coverage and detailed extraction marked NE remain incomplete.
2. **The principal shot sweep is classical probability emulation.** It matches ideal independent signed SWAP measurement statistics, but does not execute circuits. Its costs are modeled shots, with actual circuit calls zero. Actual circuit tests/probes and a validation-image demo are separately logged. There is no physical hardware result.
3. **Noise evidence is small and historical.** Three random angle pairs, not a complete medical test cohort, were simulated under full gate/readout channels. April 2021 homogeneous calibration-derived rates are not a current-device model. Synthetic line routing is not hardware-native transpilation or a physical mapping. Thermal timing, correlated error, device drift, asymmetric readout and queue time are not modeled.
4. **The allocator does not show its claimed benefit.** At the prespecified 64-shot endpoint combined allocation has higher conditional output error than uniform. The derivative objective is a first-order independent-noise approximation. Pilot uncertainty, adaptive stopping/selection bias, nonlinear propagation, floor allocation and a single shot realization per condition limit interpretation. The implemented sequential floor-and-remainder heuristic is not the exact constrained optimum. Early stopping exists but is disabled and unvalidated in central experiments.
5. **This is a short, small-model benchmark.** Ten epochs, 28-pixel images and five seeds do not establish state-of-the-art medical performance. Secondary experiments were frozen after initial central results were visible. Low-data fractions are classwise rounded. Some BreastMNIST predictions have majority-like accuracy; balanced accuracy and class-specific metrics matter. Positive class 1 is normal/benign for BreastMNIST.
6. **Five seeds offer low statistical resolution.** Minimum nonzero two-sided exact paired sign-flip p is 0.0625. Bootstrap intervals condition on a fixed test set and can disagree with exact tests. No patient/institution bootstrap or external dataset is available. No noninferiority margin was prespecified, so near-equal AUROC cannot prove guaranteed measurement savings.
7. **Some mandatory ablations remain partial.** Standard/destructive/uncompute and product/entangling comparisons are primarily circuit probes, with an additional single-validation-image standard/destructive classifier check, not independently trained circuit classifiers. Trained entangling attention, complete-test actual-circuit/noisy inference, independent shot repetitions and a complete noisy-allocation comparison remain outstanding. The isolated eight-channel, three-strength sweep covers 216 additional synthetic-pair circuits, not medical classification. Full noisy inference in the current pairwise density-matrix simulator is computationally expensive and was not represented as completed.
8. **Mitigation uses known channel parameters.** Ancilla readout inversion has measured pair-probe outcomes, including failures. It does not correct gate errors; independent calibration-shot overhead is excluded. No unmeasured ZNE benefit is claimed.
9. **Resource estimates have boundaries.** Dense MACs omit elementwise operations. Decomposed all-to-all and synthetic-line gate counts are simulator references. CPU jobs ran concurrently, so wall times cannot establish comparative speedup. Product states have an efficient classical formula.
10. **Reproducibility is local.** Source/data/environment hashes and resumable states are retained and tests pass in this environment. A clean environment installation has not been verified. PennyLane 0.45.1 uses deprecated expansion APIs, so future-version support is not implied.
11. **No clinical validation.** Official MedMNIST split identifiers do not allow an independent patient-level overlap audit. Attention heatmaps are model-attention views, not validated lesion explanations. The dashboard is research-only; its separate noise probe does not alter the displayed classifier prediction.

The initial hardware-ready title was therefore replaced. The broad acceptance criteria are not all met, and publication suitability is not established. A reproducibility/benchmarking or negative-results QML workshop is the most plausible eventual framing, subject to venue scope, deeper prior-art review and stronger circuit evidence.
